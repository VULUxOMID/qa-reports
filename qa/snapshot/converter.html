For the security-conscious

Verify your download.

Every installer we publish comes with a published SHA-256 checksum (see the download cards above). To verify yours matches, run this one-liner in a terminal after downloading:

Windows (PowerShell)
Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 "Vulu Converter Setup.exe"
macOS / Linux (Terminal)
shasum -a 256 "Vulu Converter.dmg"

The hash your machine produces should exactly match the one on the download card. If it doesn't, delete the file and re-download — it's either corrupted or tampered with.

FAQ

The common questions.

Why is Windows warning me the file isn't safe?

You'll see "Windows protected your PC" on first run. This is Microsoft's SmartScreen warning for any newly-published installer that hasn't yet built reputation — it appears for every small developer's software until enough downloads and time accumulate. We're actively working on a code-signing certificate that clears this warning instantly. Meanwhile, click More info then Run anyway. Verify the SHA-256 checksum (above) if you want additional assurance.

What about the Mac version?

The Mac build is coming — the build kit exists, we just need to run it on a Mac and put it through Apple's notarization process. Watch this page or sign up for a Vulu account to get notified when it's out.

Do I need a Vulu account to use the Converter?

No. The Converter is a standalone desktop app — it runs entirely on your machine, doesn't require signup, and doesn't talk to Vulu at all. A Vulu account only comes into play if you want the 50%-off subscriber pricing, or if you want to upload your converted MP4s to your Vulu library directly (that integration is in development).

Which sites work?

Over a thousand out of the box — YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, X (Twitter), Instagram, Facebook, SoundCloud, Twitch, and many more via the underlying open-source yt-dlp engine. Direct MP4 or HLS stream URLs also work. If a site doesn't work out of the box, you can grab the stream URL from your browser's DevTools (Network tab → filter mp4) and paste that into the Converter instead.

Is downloading videos legal?

It depends on the content and your jurisdiction. Vulu Converter is a general-purpose tool — you can and should only use it to download content you own, have permission to download, or that is clearly public-domain / Creative Commons. Downloading copyrighted material without permission may violate the source site's Terms of Service AND applicable copyright law. That's your responsibility to sort out. Vulu doesn't monitor your downloads.

Where are downloads saved?

By default: Downloads → Vulu Converter on both Windows and macOS. The folder is created the first time you use the app. Each file uses the source video's title as its name.

How do I uninstall it?

On Windows: Settings → Apps → Vulu Converter → Uninstall. On macOS: drag Vulu Converter from Applications to the Trash. Neither method touches your downloaded files — those stay put.

What's included with my Vulu subscription?

The subscription unlocks the Vulu streaming library, ad-free playback on any device, offline access, and a discounted price on the Converter ($9.99 instead of $19.99). Subscribers on the Pro tier also get the highest storage cap (75 GB / 300 videos). See the pricing page for details.
